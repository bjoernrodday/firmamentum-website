Privacy Policy

for the app “Firmamentum” · Last updated: November 2026

This is a translation. The German version is legally binding.

In short: Firmamentum does not collect any data about you. There is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no server of its own. Your location and your settings stay on your device.

1. Controller

Björn Rodday
Wellmicher Str. 77
56346 Sankt Goarshausen
Germany
Email: apps@rodday.de

2. Location

So that the sky looks the way it does at your location right now, the app asks for your location – only while you are using it and only if you agree. Your location is processed only on your device and stored there, so that the app shows the right sky straight away the next time it starts. It is not sent to me or to anyone else.

You can withdraw permission at any time in your device’s settings and choose a place by hand instead.

3. Motion sensors and compass

In the “Compass + finger” and “Sensors only” modes, the app uses your device’s motion sensors and compass so that the view follows the direction you point it in. These readings are processed only in the moment; they are not stored and not transmitted.

4. Place search

If you search for a place by name (for example “Hamburg”), the app sends the search term to Apple’s map service to look up its coordinates. Apple’s privacy policy applies to this. I receive nothing from it. If you enter coordinates instead, nothing is transmitted.

5. Settings on your device

The app stores your settings (such as the chosen sky culture, which displays are switched on, and recently used places) on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings, they may be included in your own iCloud backup; I have no access to it.

6. Links to image sources

The “About Firmamentum” section contains links to the sources of the images and data (for example ESA, ESO, NASA). If you tap a link, the page opens in your browser, and the privacy policy of that provider applies. The app never opens web pages by itself.

7. App Store and TestFlight

The app is offered through Apple’s App Store, and test versions through TestFlight. Purchases, downloads and updates are handled by Apple, which processes data under its own privacy policy. Apple gives me aggregated statistics that do not identify anyone (for example the number of downloads). If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, I can also see anonymous crash reports. In TestFlight I can additionally see which version is installed on which type of device and how often it was opened, as well as feedback you send yourself through TestFlight.

8. Contact by email

If you email me, I use your address and your message only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

9. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Because the app does not send any personal data to me, I normally hold no data about you. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Rhineland-Palatinate (www.datenschutz.rlp.de).

10. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from children either.

11. Changes

If something about the app changes that affects privacy, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
