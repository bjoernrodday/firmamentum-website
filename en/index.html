02 / Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

How do I use the app?

The app explains everything briefly the first time you open it. You can bring the explanation back at any time from the top of the display menu under “How it works”.

The app shows the wrong sky.

Firmamentum needs your location. Allow it on first launch, or later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Firmamentum. You can also search for a place by name or enter coordinates.

The view doesn’t follow my device.

Use the control button at the bottom right to choose: finger, compass + finger, or sensors only. The sky only moves with the device in the last two modes.

The Sun, Moon or stars are not quite in the right place.

This is almost always the compass. Move the device in a figure eight a few times and keep away from metal, cars and speakers. If an offset remains, swipe horizontally in “Sensors only” mode to correct the compass. The correction can be reset in the display menu.

How do I show a different time?

Use the time slider to wind the sky forwards and backwards, or enter a date. “Now” takes you back to the current time.

How do I change the language?

In the display menu under “Language”. The app is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Japanese. The new language takes effect the next time the app starts: close it completely (swipe it up in the app switcher) and open it again.

How do I protect my dark-adapted eyes?

Turn on night mode in the display menu. It turns everything red.

Does the app collect data?

No. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
